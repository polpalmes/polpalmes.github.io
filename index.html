Working paperSeptember 2026In Spanish, with English abstract

¿Muestra el mercado bursátil estadounidense señales de sobrevaloración?

Is the US stock market showing signs of overvaluation? The signals that preceded the 2000, 2008 and 2020 crashes, compared with today.

Several indicators have reached levels last seen before earlier market crises: a valuation exceeded only in 1999-2000, a ten-year Treasury yield at 2007 highs and oil above 100 dollars. The paper tests six of these signals against the run-ups to 2000, 2008 and 2020 with more than a century of data. Valuation and real interest rates clearly match past episodes, while commodities and leverage match only in part. The apparent gap between prices and earnings is largely misleading, although it hides unusually high earnings. Overall, the current market resembles 2000 more than 2008, and its margin of safety is unusually thin.

Master's thesisUPF Barcelona School of Management2026In Spanish, with English abstract

Creación de valor en fusiones y adquisiciones amistosas

Determinantes del retorno inmediato y análisis de la persistencia a largo plazo

Value creation in friendly mergers and acquisitions: announcement returns and long-run persistence. Supervised by Xavier Freixas.

Do friendly acquisitions create value for the buyer's shareholders? The thesis studies 613 deals completed between 2015 and 2021 by listed acquirers in North America and Europe, combining an event study around the announcement with a calendar-time portfolio analysis based on the Fama-French three- and five-factor models. The immediate market reaction is neutral on average, but cash-financed, cross-border and high-liquidity deals earn higher returns, while larger deals earn less. Over the following years, acquirers underperform their risk benchmark by 2.96% a year at 36 months, with a stronger effect among European acquirers.

About

My master's thesis, supervised by Xavier Freixas, studied value creation in friendly mergers and acquisitions. I am a CFA Level I candidate, sitting the exam in November 2026.

I work in external audit at Grant Thornton in Barcelona. Before that, I worked in management control and financial analysis at Stikets, an international e-commerce company based in Igualada.

My main interests are equity valuation, M&A and portfolio management. I work mainly with Excel and Python, and speak Catalan, Spanish and English. The best way to reach me is through LinkedIn.
